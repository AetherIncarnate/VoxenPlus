Touch

  • TAP sets the time of the highlighted word. In Hold mode, keep your finger down for as long as the word is sung.
  • Tap a word to jump to it and make it the next one to time.
  • Long-press a line to edit it.
  • Double-tap a word to split it into syllables (when syllables are on).
  • Undo and redo are the arrows next to TAP, or in the top bar.
  • Swipe up on the bottom bar to open the tools.
Taps go to the selected layer. B switches.

Editor

Syllable sync beta

Splits words into syllables, so each syllable gets its own time. Words are looked up in several online dictionaries, and plain spelling rules take over when you are offline. Fix any word by hand. Only single words are sent to a dictionary, never your whole lyrics, and answers are kept in this browser. Words are cut the way they are sung: fee·ling, drea·ming, figh·ting.
